How it works

The dataset

A hand-curated list of landmark companies from 1939 to 2023: the most valuable, the category-definers, and the ones that changed daily life. Every founder is linked to each college they attended, marked as an undergraduate degree, a graduate degree, or left without finishing.

The four signals

  • Value created: sum of log₂(1 + peak value in $B) across alumni companies, so one $3T giant counts about as much as a few $100B companies, not 30 of them.
  • Companies: how many landmark companies alumni founded.
  • Impact: sum of each company's 1–5 impact score (5 = changed how the world works).
  • Per student: impact divided by enrollment (plus a 20,000-student allowance so tiny colleges with one famous founder don't dominate).

Score and tiers

Each signal is scaled so the leader scores 100, then blended with your weights and rescaled so the top college scores 100. Tiers: S ≥ 60, A ≥ 30, B ≥ 15, C ≥ 6, D below.

Read with care

This is a curated sample, not a census: it favours famous, mostly US companies, and impact scores are judgment calls. Correlation isn't causation; elite colleges also select for ambition. Values are approximate as of late 2025.
